VST3 plugin and standalone app  ·  Windows 10 and 11  ·  Nothing to install on the phone

A microphone you already own

No cable, no interface, no driver. Just the phone in your pocket and the Wi-Fi you already have.

Nothing to install on the phone

The phone side runs in its browser. Scan the QR code in the plugin window and tap Start.

Takes without gaps

Every recording is saved exactly as the phone sent it. A Wi-Fi hiccup you heard live is not in the take.

Press record, like any mic

In Ableton Live the take lands on the track as a clip, right where you started recording.

Wi-Fi, hotspot or USB

Works on your home network, on the phone's own hotspot, or over USB tethering when there is no Wi-Fi.

Private by design

Audio travels encrypted, directly from phone to computer on your local network. No account, no cloud.

No audio driver

The audio arrives inside the plugin, so it works with whatever audio device your DAW is using.

Recording in three steps

From installing to your first take in a couple of minutes.

1

Add MobiMic to a track

2

Guide

Everything you need to set up and record.

Install

  1. Download the installer and run it.
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins if MobiMic doesn't appear.

The installer adds the VST3 plugin, the standalone app, the Ableton Live helper, and a firewall rule so your phone can reach the plugin.

Connect your phone

  1. Put the phone and the computer on the same network: the same Wi-Fi, the phone's hotspot, or USB tethering.
  2. Drag MobiMic onto an audio track, or open the app.
  3. Scan the QR code with the phone's camera.
  4. The first time, the browser warns that the connection is not private. Tap Advanced, then Proceed. Why?
  5. Allow the microphone and tap Start.

Keep the phone's screen on while you use it.

Record in Ableton Live

  1. One time only: restart Live, open Preferences → Link, Tempo & MIDI, and choose MobiMic in a free Control Surface slot. Leave Input and Output on None.
  2. Put MobiMic on an audio track. The window should say Ableton helper on.
  3. In the Arrangement, press record, perform, and stop.
  4. The take appears on that track, starting where recording began.

You don't need to arm the track. Clips already on it keep playing through MobiMic.

Record in other DAWs

  1. Record in your DAW as usual. MobiMic saves a take while the DAW is recording.
  2. When you stop, the take appears in the plugin window. Drag it onto a track.

If your DAW doesn't tell plugins when it is recording, use the Capture button to start and stop a take yourself. Takes are also kept in Documents\MobiMic\Takes.

Use the standalone app

  1. Open MobiMic from the Start menu and connect your phone.
  2. Press Record, perform, then press Stop.
  3. Drag the take from the window into any app, or open the takes folder.

To choose speakers or headphones, use Options in the title bar.

Get the timing right

Sound takes a moment to travel from the phone to the computer, so MobiMic shifts each take earlier by the Offset amount.

  1. Turn on the metronome and record yourself clapping on the beat.
  2. If the claps sit late, raise Offset in settings. If they sit early, lower it.

Settings

Tap the round button at the top right of the window.

SettingWhat it does
GainVolume of the phone signal, for what you hear and what is recorded. It is applied on the phone, so boosting adds no noise. If the waveform turns red it is too loud.
BufferHow much audio is held back to ride out network hiccups. Higher means fewer dropouts and more delay when listening live. 120 ms suits most networks.
Offset plugin onlyHow far each take is shifted earlier to cancel the phone's delay.
Hear the phone livePlays the phone through the track or your speakers. Turn it off if the delay is distracting; takes are still recorded.
Capture while the DAW records plugin onlySaves a take automatically whenever the DAW is recording.
Sound clean-up on the phoneNoise suppression, auto gain and echo cancellation are made for speech. Switch them off for singing and instruments.

Questions

And what to try when something isn't working.

Is it really free?

Yes. MobiMic is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence. The code is on GitHub.

Which phones work?

Chrome on Android is the tested setup. iPhone (Safari) is untested and may refuse the connection.

Which DAWs work?

Any DAW on Windows that loads VST3 plugins. Automatic placing of takes on the timeline is for Ableton Live 12; in other DAWs you drag the take in.

Why does the phone show a security warning?

Phone browsers only allow microphone access on encrypted pages. MobiMic creates its own certificate on your computer for this, and since no authority has vouched for it, the browser asks you to confirm once per address. The audio is encrypted and never leaves your local network.

Can I hear myself while I sing?

Not comfortably. The phone's audio reaches the computer roughly a tenth of a second late, which is fine for recording but too much for monitoring your own voice. Turn off Hear the phone live, listen to the backing track in headphones, and add effects to the take afterwards.

Will a Wi-Fi dropout ruin my recording?

The live sound can drop out if the network stalls for longer than the buffer. The saved take cannot: it is written from the complete stream as it arrives, so every sample is there in order. The exception is a connection that drops entirely and reconnects, which leaves a gap.

The phone says "This site can't be reached"
  • Check the phone and computer are on the same network. Guest and public Wi-Fi often stop devices talking to each other; the phone's hotspot or USB tethering works.
  • If the computer is on several networks, click Use another address in the window and scan again.
  • In Windows Security → Firewall & network protection → Public network, make sure Blocks all incoming connections is not ticked. It overrides the rule MobiMic's installer adds.
I recorded in Ableton but no clip appeared
  • The window should say Ableton helper on. If it says off, restart Live and choose MobiMic as a Control Surface in Preferences → Link, Tempo & MIDI.
  • Record in the Arrangement view, with MobiMic on an audio track.
  • Check Capture while the DAW records is on in settings.
  • The take is always kept in Documents\MobiMic\Takes, so you can still drag it in.
The sound is choppy

Raise Buffer in settings, move closer to the router or phone, or use USB tethering. For anything you want to keep, use the saved take.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. MobiMic is Windows only for now.
